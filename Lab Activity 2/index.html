About the Organization

The Student IT Association is a community of aspiring developers, designers, and tech enthusiasts dedicated to growing practical skills in software development. We host workshops, hackathons, and peer mentoring sessions throughout the year to help members build real-world projects and launch their careers in technology.

Membership Requirements

  • Must be currently enrolled in an IT or technology-related degree program.
  • Maintain a passion for software development, networking, or computer science.
  • Active participation in monthly workshops and tech seminars.
